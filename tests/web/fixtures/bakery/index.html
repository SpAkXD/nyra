Our story

About us

Baked with patience, shared with love

Hana's Bakery began in 1989 in a tiny kitchen in Brno. Today the whole family bakes together, using local flour, real butter and no shortcuts.

Our dough rests overnight, our ovens start before sunrise, and every loaf is shaped by hand.
