FAQ

Questions we hear often

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. We accept new adult patients and children from 3 years. Book a first check-up with the form above.

Which insurance companies do you work with?

All Czech public health insurers, including VZP, ČPZP and OZP.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card, an ID and a list of any medicines you take.

Does dental hygiene hurt?

Usually not. If your gums are sensitive, we use a numbing gel at no extra cost.

How do I cancel an appointment?

Call us at least 24 hours ahead so that someone else can take your time.
