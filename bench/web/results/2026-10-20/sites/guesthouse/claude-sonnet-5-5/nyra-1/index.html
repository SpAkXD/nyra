FAQ

Good to know

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is served from 7:30 to 10:00 in the mill room or on the terrace.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

Yes. Tell us your arrival time and we will leave your key in a code box at the door.

Do you have parking?

We have eight free spaces in our courtyard. There is no need to book one.

Can I cancel my booking?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. After that we charge the first night.
